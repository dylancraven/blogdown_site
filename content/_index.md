We study how biodiversity varies across space and time, how it responds to global change,
and how these dynamics shape ecosystem resilience. We are based in the [Department 
of Ecosystems and the Environment](https://agronomia.uc.cl/facultad/departamentos/ecosistemas-y-medio-ambiente/)
at the [Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile](https://www.uc.cl/), and collaborate with  
the [Data Observatory Foundation](https://www.dataobservatory.net/).

## Our research  

### Functional biogeography  

We investigate how functional traits  shape species distributions, 
community assembly, and biodiversity patterns across environmental and anthropogenic gradients.

### Global change and ecosystem resilience  

We investigate how biological invasions, pollution, climate change, and land-use 
change alter biodiversity, ecosystem functioning, and resilience.

### Biodiversity monitoring  

We develop open, data-driven approaches to assess and monitor biodiversity, integrating 
species occurrence and abundance data with [functional trait data](https://www.rasgos.cl) 
to support biodiversity research and conservation.
