I am an ecologist interested in understanding how functional traits shape biodiversity patterns and ecosystem responses to global change.  

## Research interests 

- Functional biogeography  
- Global change ecology  
- Biodiversity monitoring  

## Selected initiatives

### Rasgos-CL

A [functional trait database](https://www.rasgos.cl) of Chilean woody plants that provides open-access trait information to advance biodiversity research and conservation.

### Biodiversity monitoring and open science  

Developing data-driven approaches to improve biodiversity assessment and support conservation decision-making.
