I am a biologist and my research focuses on the patterns and processes that shape biodiversity in the face of environmental change and ecosystem management, integrating ecological and socio-environmental perspectives to contribute to its conservation and sustainability.

## Research interests 

- Functional ecology
- Beta diversity and biotic homogenization
- Community ecology
- Forests and sustainable forest management
- Society-nature interactions and sustainability
- Biodiversity conservation and global change
